Act 06 · The Lookbook

How it will look. How it will feel.

Working Title
"Setting the Standard"
Visual
Close on the laser line finding its mark, the materials, and hands on. Wide showing the site being run; tight enough to show the precision. No stock office shots or staged handshakes. We're running the process.
Pace
Measured and confident, not fast-cut hype. Cut to the rhythm of the process itself.
Sound
Your voice walking through the process, recorded on the day and cut as voiceover over the work. Natural sound from the laser and the tools underneath, with sparse, confident scoring. Subtitled throughout, so it still lands for the merchants and fitters who meet it scrolling with the sound off.
Structure
Opens on arrival on site. Builds through the live template: the laser, the material, the read. Closes on the finished result and the standard statement: this is how we work.

Act 07 · Production Schedule

How we make it.

One shoot day in October banks the quarter. Everything after is rollout, reach and review.

October
Foundation Shoot
One full shoot day on site, with the exact date confirmed within October. You running a live template, the laser, the process, captured to deliver the whole quarter's material in a single day: the hero capability film, 6–8 stills for site and profile, plus a full profile and website audit and bio rewrite.
November
Rollout
No new shoot. Distribution from the material banked in October, plus one capability cut built specifically for kitchen fitters and merchants.
December
Reach & Review
No new shoot. Continued distribution, plus one further cut, either an employer-brand piece or a second capability angle for housing and refurb. Reassessed together at month end, based on the assets delivered and the trajectory of the business.
